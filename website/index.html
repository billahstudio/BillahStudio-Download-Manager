Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BillahStudio Download Manager free to use?
Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, ads, or paywalled features.
What operating systems are supported?
Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit Intel & AMD).
How does the browser video downloader work?
The application bundles a lightweight browser extension that communicates securely with the desktop app via native messaging. When it detects video streams on web pages, an overlay button appears allowing 1-click accelerated download.
Can I pause and resume downloads?
Yes. Any server that supports HTTP Range requests can be paused and resumed at any time, even after restarting your computer.
